3 Connect to Wi-Fi and Domoticz

    
Tell the device your Wi-Fi and where Domoticz is. Fill in both the first time; later you can send only the part that changed. The Wi-Fi password and the token travel over the USB cable to the device only. They are not sent to any server, not stored by this page, and cleared from the page after a successful send.
